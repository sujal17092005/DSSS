BINARY SEARCH TREE - EASY EXAMPLE

Insert these values in this order:
50 30 70 20 40 60 80

TREE DIAGRAM:
             50
           /    \
         30      70
        /  \    /  \
       20  40  60  80

INORDER: Left -> Root -> Right
20 30 40 50 60 70 80
(In a BST, inorder prints values in ascending order.)

PREORDER: Root -> Left -> Right
50 30 20 40 70 60 80

POSTORDER: Left -> Right -> Root
20 40 30 60 80 70 50

LEVEL ORDER: Visit one level at a time, left to right
50 30 70 20 40 60 80

The C program in 17_BST_All_Traversals_With_Diagram.txt
prints the BST sideways and prints all three DFS traversals.
